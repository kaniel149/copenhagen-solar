# Foundation research — 7 September 2026

Eight original trilingual foundation lessons. All numerical property cases are invented teaching assumptions, not customer forecasts. Sources below were searched and opened on 7 September 2026; undated documents are not represented as newly published. Source facts are narrowly used; the energy ledgers, exercises and handover models are original applications.

- **base-pv** — [Solar Photovoltaic Cell Basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-photovoltaic-cell-basics) — US Department of Energy; Undated. Scope: PV conversion and cell technologies; a technical explanation, not Thai installation rules.
- **base-inverter** — [Solar Integration: Inverters and Grid Services Basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-integration-inverters-and-grid-services-basics) — US Department of Energy; Undated. Scope: DC/AC conversion and grid-following versus grid-forming functions. Actual capabilities require the product manual.
- **base-efficiency** — [Solar Photovoltaic Performance and Efficiency Basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-photovoltaic-performance-and-efficiency-basics) — US Department of Energy; Undated. Scope: Efficiency, temperature and optical losses; does not predict a specific property.
- **base-units** — [Measuring electricity](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/measuring-electricity.php) — US Energy Information Administration; Undated. Scope: Power, energy and meter concepts; no Thai tariff information.
- **base-storage** — [Solar Integration: Solar Energy and Storage Basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-integration-solar-energy-and-storage-basics) — US Department of Energy; Undated. Scope: Energy storage power, energy capacity and conversion losses; examples here are original training assumptions.
- **base-battery** — [Batteries — Solar Consumer Guide](https://www.energy.gov.au/solar/get-know-solar-technology/batteries) — Australian Government; Undated; includes 2025 program information. Scope: Backup configuration, usable energy and power distinctions only. Australian subsidies, accreditation and rules are not applied to Thailand.
- **base-model** — [PVWatts V8 API](https://developer.nlr.gov/docs/solar/pvwatts/v8/) — National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR); Version 8. Scope: Model inputs and estimated output; input assumptions and local shading still require review.
- **base-design** — [Design considerations — Solar Consumer Guide](https://www.energy.gov.au/solar/solar-system-design/design-considerations) — Australian Government; Undated. Scope: General shade, roof and design considerations; network rules remain jurisdiction-specific.
- **base-climate** — [Fact Sheet: Optimisation of Photovoltaic Systems for Different Climates](https://iea-pvps.org/fact-sheets/fs-t13-optimisation-climates-2025/) — IEA PVPS Task 13; August 2025. Scope: Climate-related design and reliability stressors, not a certified site-specific design.
- **base-controls** — [Hierarchy of Controls](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/hierarchy-of-controls/index.html) — CDC / NIOSH; 10 April 2024. Scope: General prevention framework; not a statement of Thai legal duties or training authorization.
- **base-falls** — [Green Job Hazards — Solar Energy: Falls](https://www.osha.gov/green-jobs/solar/falls) — US OSHA; Undated. Scope: Recognition of roof, ladder and skylight hazards. US height thresholds are not imported into Thai practice.
- **base-pea** — [ประกาศและข้อกำหนดต่าง ๆ เกี่ยวกับการเชื่อมต่อระบบโครงข่ายไฟฟ้า](https://www.pea.co.th/business-partner/regulation) — Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA); Index includes 30 June 2026 notice. Scope: Official interconnection-document index, with separate generation and EV topics. Confirm the applicable document and project route with PEA.
- **base-maintain** — [Look after your solar system](https://www.energy.gov.au/solar/use-your-solar-system/look-after-your-solar-system) — Australian Government; Undated. Scope: Hazard awareness and the need for qualified maintenance. Australian accreditation and suggested intervals are not Thai requirements.
- **base-roof-safety** — [Guide to managing the risks of rooftop solar installation work](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/doc/guide-managing-risks-rooftop-solar-installation-work) — Safe Work Australia; September 2025; revision 1.1. Scope: General roof, electrical, battery, heat and emergency-planning guidance. Australian legal provisions do not establish Thai work authorization.
- **base-lifecycle** — [Life Cycle of Photovoltaic Systems: Operate and Maintain an Existing Photovoltaic System](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/life-cycle-photovoltaic-systems-operate-and-maintain-existing-photovoltaic-system) — US Department of Energy / FEMP; Undated. Scope: O&M planning, monitoring and weather-response concepts, not Thai contract or electrical law.
