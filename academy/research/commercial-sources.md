## Curriculum map

Sales: 1 discovery and decision makers; 2 bill/load evidence; 3 qualification and survey handoff; 4 solution/value boundaries; 5 tariffs/export certainty; 6 comparable proposals/warranties; 7 objections/contract risk; 8 CRM/negotiation; 9 customer-to-operations capstone.

Finance: 1 meter-to-money ledger; 2 self-consumption cash flows; 3 class/date-specific PEA bill; 4 lifecycle costs; 5 NPV/IRR/LCOE; 6 debt/DSCR/liquidity; 7 tax/FX/milestones; 8 sensitivity/procurement risk; 9 investment approval memo.

## Editorial and assessment boundaries

Every lesson separates evidence from assumptions, uses a distinct island-property case, includes an exercise and model solution, and has five explained questions. Capstones require human assessment of evidence, financial calculations and honest communication; quiz completion alone does not authorize site work or certify professional competence. Exact tariff examples use September2026 class1.1.2 base charges and period-scoped Ft only, with VAT explicitly excluded unless separately validated. Export revenue is zero in the base training case when no executed applicable purchase agreement is evidenced; this is a conservative modeling convention, not a claim that export is prohibited.

Clinical, legal and hazardous technical procedures are outside these commercial lessons. Structural, electrical, fire, permitting and tax questions go to the responsible qualified reviewer with the underlying documents. Existing technical diagrams need accuracy validation before being relied on for installation or design.
