# Bustan Academy — planning, permitting and leadership source dossier

Research date: 7 September 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). Teaching languages: English, Hebrew, Thai. Sources are primary issuer/authority documents or manufacturer/laboratory documentation. All scenarios and management models are original training assumptions. Training is not a professional license, authority approval, installation authorization or a performance guarantee.

## Decisions that must stay separate

1. PEA technical grid interconnection and its review/inspection/permission to operate.
2. Eligibility, allocation and signed electricity sale agreement under a specific purchase program. The PEA residential program announced 30 June 2026 accepts applications from 1 July 2026 for Type 1 residential accounts with matching meter-owner/applicant details. Confirm capacity and remaining allocation for each project. Do not apply it to commercial resorts merely because guests sleep there.
3. Self-use/non-sale operation: no-export controls and their failure behavior are engineering controls, not an exemption from PEA connection review. PEA exposes a separate DG-SELF application route.
4. ERC generation licensing or exemption notification, and the separate controlled-energy พค.2 decision. ERC’s directly read 25 April 2025 infographic distinguishes <200 kVA and 200–<1,000 kVA with พค.2. Apparent AC kVA is not DC module kWp. The infographic publication date is not the underlying law’s effective date. Confirm latest filing route and any generation/addition aggregation with ERC/PEA/DEDE for the actual installation. No numerical licensing entitlement is promised in the curriculum.
5. Local building/roof alteration classification, structural adequacy, owner permission and professional design signoff. Ministerial Regulation 72 (2568) is signed 17 November 2025 and Gazette dated 19 November 2025, volume142 part76A pages8–9. The municipality’s primary reproduction says total installed solar-system load in any area ≤20 kg/m² (not a whole-roof average or the existing roof weight) is not building alteration. This is not a structural adequacy certificate or a blanket exemption from electrical/other approvals. Exact commencement interpretation and application to the plot, occupancy, roof works, additional structures and local ordinances must be confirmed with the responsible Koh Phangan building authority. Do not reuse the superseded 160m² framing as current.
6. Workplace electrical inspection/certification and record reporting, distinct from PEA energization. The DLPW current record notice was signed23 December2024, Gazette13 January2025, effective14 January2025. It includes annual workplace inspection and reporting within30 days by the required qualified registrant/licensed entity. Applicability to the actual business and inspector registration must be confirmed. The general electrical safety regulation is Gazette6 February2015; its express commencement clause says after60 days from Gazette publication. Do not confuse the2026 file upload directory with legal enactment.

## Local confirmation register

- PEA Koh Phangan office: account class, connection configuration, current inverter/model listing, protection and no-export acceptance, inspection witness/energization sequence, program capacity and application route, storage/EV modifications.
- ERC/DEDE: actual aggregate inverter kVA and scope, filing channel, exemption/controlled-energy obligations, current underlying effective legal instruments. DEDE page https://www.dede.go.th/articles?id=676 and citizen manual https://www.dede.go.th/uploads/O11_16c9e882ee.pdf were attempted; content fetch failed/returned a shell. They are leads, not a basis for exact current procedure claims.
- Local building official and appropriately licensed structural/electrical engineer: plot jurisdiction, roof exception applicability, professional scope, load paths/wind/corrosion, additions/change-of-use and required submissions. No property-specific ruling obtained.
- TISI: verify exact model and current license/status and whether the relevant standard is legally compulsory. A2025 consultation page was encountered but not relied on as an enacted mandate. A specific actual2022 module license was read as a record example only.
- DLPW provincial safety authority: applicable workplace inspection, training and fall-protection requirements; registration and competency. Course quizzes are not a substitute.
- Manufacturer: exact installed model, serial, regional warranty, current manual revision, firmware/control compatibility and transport rules for batteries. No universal battery location, RCD type, fuse factor or cable size is prescribed.

## Curriculum design

Design and permitting:1 Survey into a design brief;2 Shade and yield scenarios;3 Supervised string design;4 Cable/protection/earthing decisions;5 Battery and EV integration;6 SLD and evidence pack;7 Six separate approval decisions;8 As-built and commissioning acceptance;9 Permit-ready island property capstone.

Leadership:1 RACI and stage gates;2 Island schedule and constraints;3 Cost/procurement/logistics;4 Safety and quality governance;5 Subcontractor accountability;6 Change and risk control;7 Commissioning evidence and payment gates;8 Warranty/service/staffing KPIs;9 Project recovery and operating-review capstone.

Each lesson has four substantive sections with equivalent teaching in all three languages, an original worked island example, a different exercise with model solution, a checklist and five explained questions. Human capstone assessment evaluates evidence and judgment; a passing quiz alone grants no practical qualification.
